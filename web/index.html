How the targets were built

Future price. Future = Price × (1 + r)5. The rate r starts at 8% a year and is adjusted for infrastructure (up to ±2%), under-construction status (+1%), age over 25 years (−1%), each amenity (+0.2%) and villas (+0.5%).

Good investment. A property gets one point each for: price per sq ft at or below its city's median · 3 or more BHK · ready to move · infrastructure score ≥ 0.5 · growth ≥ 8%. A score of 3 or more is rated good.
